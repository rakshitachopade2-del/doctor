Stale policy drafts and appointment previews require explicit fresh review. Malformed success, later rejection and wrong-actor receipts retain the same uncertain command until valid replay. Staff read-only permissions, recoverable history failures and obsolete preview/run/history authorization responses are tested across navigation away and Back. Mobile views receive 320-pixel accessibility checks.

The local and disposable Docker production checks verify the reminder worker starts with a disabled policy, then create a synthetic linked-patient visit and an unroutable visit. They enable a 72-hour policy, reject a stale preview, record one exact patient notice, verify unchanged care-summary revision and private-history access, and replay without duplicates. Duration changes do not resend; a new appointment time creates a new reminder, returning to the old time does not, and cancellation preserves historical receipts. Replaying an earlier policy command returns its original change alongside the later disabled policy. All smoke data and containers are temporary; no deployment was performed.

Axe checks in the browser suite report no detectable WCAG 2 A/AA and 2.1 AA violations on public discovery/sign-in, the clinic dashboard/booking dialog, mobile patient care/records, account security, and desktop/mobile scheduling, clinical workup and amendments, and mobile patient investigations/procedures/PDF reports, follow-up reviews and assignments, activity, waitlist workflows, procedure-linked invoices, administrator reports, expense history, image annotation editing/viewing and patient care summaries and inventory history/stock forms and staff/patient correction records at 320 pixels. The patient record layout, knowledge editor/history, recurring visit preview/history and saved-summary archive/document and series cancellation/rescheduling preview/history and appointment-reminder controls/current patient visits are also checked at 320 pixels wide. Additional development checks covered clinic records, follow-ups, billing, knowledge, settings, and invoice/article/check-in dialogs. Automated checks are bounded to those rendered states; they do not establish comprehensive accessibility certification.

# Product decisions and concept coverage

The supplied concept is source material, not an instruction to make unsupported medical or commercial claims. Its unresolved items remain unresolved unless this implementation establishes a concrete operational behavior.

## Release scope

The platform includes the care workspace, configurable availability and account lifecycle management, structured investigations, procedure records, private PDF reports, consultation amendments, clinician follow-up reviews, a private in-app activity inbox, confirmed waitlist booking, assignable follow-up ownership, procedure-linked invoices, administrator reports, an immutable expense ledger, versioned image annotations and patient-facing care-summary export, consumable inventory and attributed patient-detail/allergy corrections and billing-entry corrections, a clinic-wide investigation review worklist with assignable responsibility, optional room/equipment reservations for existing appointments, bounded staff record search with complete stored audit-history navigation, account-based staff attendance with audited corrections and administrator timesheets, custom visit lengths with retained scheduling history, an operational worklist for assigning existing procedure plans to booked visits, optional authenticator sign-in, operational follow-up escalation and a versioned clinician-authored procedure catalogue.

## Decisions made for these releases

- One deployed clinic has four explicit roles: administrator, doctor, receptionist and patient. Doctor accounts are tied to professional profiles. Administrators can manage clinic operations, but must also have a linked doctor profile to author clinical notes or care plans.
- Local accounts avoid dependency on an external identity provider. Production never accepts a demo-seeded database. New administrator-provisioned staff and patient portal accounts must replace their initial password before accessing private workspace data. Self-registered patients choose their own password during registration.
- Administrators can suspend or restore accounts without deleting care records. Role, doctor-profile and active-status changes end the affected account’s sessions and invalidate its outstanding recovery grants. Patient accounts cannot be converted into staff accounts or relinked to another patient. At least one active administrator must remain, and administrators cannot suspend themselves.
- Account holders can inspect their active sessions and end other sessions. Password changes keep the current session and end the others. Sessions have a fixed twelve-hour lifetime; browser labels are descriptive and are not verified device identities.
- Recovery is a clinic-assisted process: an administrator confirms identity and obtains a one-use link that expires after thirty minutes. The administrator shares it privately outside the app. Only the token digest is stored. Redeeming the link changes the password, ends all prior sessions and requires a fresh sign-in. No email or SMS is sent automatically, and the identity-confirmation checkbox is not automated identity verification.
- Bookable hours come from each doctor’s weekly schedule. The default is Monday–Saturday, 09:00–18:00 IST, in thirty-minute slots; it can be replaced with other days, multiple sessions, mode-specific hours and 15/20/30/45/60-minute appointment lengths. The current booking horizon is 180 days. The public clinic-hours description is informational and does not configure availability.
- Slots are authoritative on the server. Transactional overlap checks protect both doctor and patient time, including appointments with different lengths. Time off can cover one doctor or the entire clinic, for full days or a repeated daily time interval. A schedule change or closure that invalidates future bookings is rejected; it never automatically cancels those bookings.
- Appointment duration is recorded with the booking. Changing weekly availability preserves existing appointments and their duration. Explicitly rescheduling an ordinary appointment selects a current slot and adopts the selected doctor's current appointment length. Staff-selected custom visits retain their saved length through patient or staff rescheduling, until staff explicitly restore the default policy. Calendar exports use the recorded length. Earlier records without a duration are interpreted as thirty minutes.
- Waitlist requests record a clinician, consultation mode, date window and reason. A patient may have one waiting request per clinician and mode. Staff confirm the patient agrees to a specific available slot before booking; patients confirm their own booking. The appointment and request transition save in one transaction. Requests do not reserve slots or send external offers. Withdrawal and expiry preserve the original request; cancelling a resulting appointment does not reopen it.
- Staff can preview 2–12 appointments at weekly, fortnightly or four-weekly intervals and confirm patient agreement for the complete set. Every exact visit must fit the existing 180-day horizon and doctor/patient availability; the application never skips conflicts or creates a partial series. Current names, fees, duration and availability are reviewed again after a stale preview. An immutable origin retains the original booked snapshots and private staff note; later reschedules and cancellations remain individual actions. Series booking does not allocate rooms, attach procedures, issue invoices or create an ongoing automatic schedule.
- Status-only appointment changes preserve the booked fee and clinician name, including check-in, consultation progression and cancellation. Explicit rescheduling adopts the selected clinician’s current profile fee and name, shown during booking. Invoices remain separate, explicitly recorded charges.
- Each physical room or shared equipment unit has capacity one. Staff optionally allocate it to an existing appointment; new and waitlist bookings are unallocated. Reservations span the recorded visit interval and move atomically with explicit rescheduling. Administrators manage operational metadata and maintenance windows. Cancellation/no-show releases capacity; completed records retain the scheduled interval. No live occupancy, cleaning buffer, equipment suitability or maintenance certification is inferred. Resource history and reasons stay staff-only.
- Appointment states follow a defined transition graph. Patients may cancel or reschedule their own scheduled appointments; they cannot progress clinic states.
- Staff can correct current demographics with a reason and retained before/after history. Reception sees only demographic information; linked clinicians separately update explicit allergy status and details. Allergy changes and their reasons are shared with the patient, while demographic correction reasons remain staff-only. The two revision streams are independent. These updates preserve login identity and historical document names.
- Internal visit notes and their amendments are clinician-only. A correction adds a dated, attributed amendment with a reason; it does not overwrite the original record. Patients receive their structured care plans, shared history, images, PDF reports, investigation requests/results and published reviews, procedure information, messages, appointments and invoices.
- Investigations separate an order, a submitted result and a clinician’s review. Submitted notes and attached reports are not automatically interpreted. Only linked clinicians can write reviews, and patients see a review only after explicit publication. A supplemental result changes the request back to awaiting review of the latest submission; previous results and published reviews remain in the history.
- The clinical investigation queue uses the latest result and its reviews to identify work. A private review completes that result's review task while its publication state remains separate; a supplemental result reopens review. Original requested dates mark missing results past that date only and imply no clinical urgency. The original ordering doctor stays fixed; the current owner or an administrator can hand responsibility to an active linked clinician with immutable private history. Ownership is not an access boundary. New results notify the current owner's eligible accounts, with clinic fallback if none are active.
- Investigation review forms require the full current source revision and a retry UUID. Concurrent source changes require explicit context review while preserving the draft; uncertain responses retain the exact attempt. Patient workflows and summaries never include handover reasons, assignment records or internal revision tokens. New publication on a cancelled order is rejected; retrying an already published review preserves its original publication date.
- Report attachments must belong to the same patient as the investigation. PDFs up to 5 MB retain their original bytes, file fingerprint, uploader and date. Format checks and private attachment downloads are implemented; malware scanning, OCR and clinical content extraction are not. PDF access is a core record capability independent of the optional image-capture switch.
- Procedure information records the clinician’s indication, expected benefits, risks, alternatives, aftercare and cost. The patient can agree or decline through the portal after reviewing that information. A clinician can record an in-person or phone decision only with explicit confirmation and a discussion note. All decisions remain in order with their author and method.
- Procedure decisions are documentation of a discussion and choice, not a validated legal e-signature or a complete consent framework. Completion requires the latest recorded choice to be agreement, a clinician-entered outcome and aftercare, and a valid linked appointment where one exists. Completed/cancelled procedures reject further decisions and cannot be overwritten through the application. A planned procedure can receive a revised patient decision.
- Clinical instructions are entered by a clinician. No medication recommendation, clinical probability, image-based score or future-outcome image is generated.
- Check-ins are explicitly patient-reported. One per care plan/day can be corrected; changes are audited. They are not presented as observed adherence or clinical efficacy.
- Follow-up worklists separate due plans, new patient reports, upcoming reviews, completed plans and all plans. A plan initially belongs to its author; its current owner or an administrator can transfer follow-up responsibility to another clinician with an active linked account. Every transfer records a reason and attribution, preserves the original clinical author, and changes the My plans filter and check-in notification routing. Ownership is not an access boundary; other authorized clinicians may still review the plan. Doctors default to their own plans and administrators to all clinic plans.
- A clinical follow-up review preserves the assessment, patient-facing instructions, outcome, clinician and date. Continuing follow-up and requesting a visit require a next review date; completion ends new patient check-ins. Instructions are shared immediately by the explicit save action. Assessments and report snapshots remain clinician-only.
- Reviews preserve the exact versions of patient check-ins considered. A same-day correction becomes unreviewed again. A revision check rejects a save if another review, a patient report or ownership changed after the form loaded; the clinician can refresh the context while retaining the draft. Merely opening the record or marking activity read does not clear the review worklist.
- The current status and next review date reflect the latest review. Original treatment instructions, medications, treatment end date and original follow-up date remain intact. Continuing follow-up does not extend a prescription; requesting a visit does not reserve an appointment. Compatibility status updates remain attributed history events but do not count as a clinical review of patient reports.
- Pausing the follow-up module stops new check-ins, clinical follow-up reviews and messages. Previous plans, check-ins, reviews and messages stay readable. The compatibility status endpoint can still close or reopen a plan; the current UI uses a documented clinical review for completion.
- Activity is a recipient-specific in-app inbox with explicit read state and links to the source record. Eligible patient accounts receive appointment and care updates. Patient messages reach eligible clinical accounts; care-plan reports target the current follow-up owner and investigation results target the current owner’s eligible accounts, with a clinic-team fallback when the owner is unavailable. Reception does not receive clinical activity.
- Activity starts with new events after this release; existing records and events before an eligible account was available are not backfilled. Opening a record or the inbox does not automatically mark updates read. Mark all read applies through the displayed snapshot and preserves later arrivals. No unread/read state is a claim of external delivery, clinical assessment, or patient adherence.
- Optional follow-up escalation uses days after the effective review date in Asia/Kolkata. The default policy is disabled, with owner/team thresholds of 3/7 days. Administrators can preview exact recipients and create notices; the running server also checks at startup and every 15 minutes unless configured for manual-only operation. One immutable notice per review episode/stage prevents duplicate sends. Check-ins, ownership transfers and policy edits do not reset an episode. Missing eligible accounts leave a plan eligible for a later check. Policy, receipt and notice histories remain private to clinicians; notices do not alter clinical review revisions or patient summaries.
- Inbox updates are loaded when the app is opened, refreshed or regains focus. Email, SMS, WhatsApp and push notifications remain unconfigured. Recorded escalation notices describe overdue review dates and selected recipients; they do not imply external delivery, clinical urgency, patient adherence or clinician review.
- Image annotations are separate immutable overlay revisions tied to the original file fingerprint. Linked clinicians add bounded rectangle, ellipse or arrow marks with notes, using normalized coordinates rather than physical measurements. Saves are private; only the latest saved revision can be newly published after explicit confirmation. Patients see published versions only, while later private drafts remain invisible. Sharing neither changes the original bytes nor diagnoses the image.
- Care summaries use the same patient-facing projection for clinical staff and patients. Internal notes/amendments, unpublished interpretations, private annotations, follow-up assessments/report snapshots/assignment reasons, messages and file bytes are excluded. JSON downloads require a matching content revision; printing preserves the displayed snapshot. Original care dates and instructions remain distinct from later reviews.
- Image agreement covers private treatment use only. There is no automatic publishing path from a clinical image to the public website.
- Draft knowledge is private. Publication requires an explicit clinician review action. Editing published material returns it to draft unless it is explicitly approved again.
- Knowledge authoring saves content, publication state, immutable versions/change events, retry receipts and audit evidence together. Editors use current revision tokens and retain drafts when another author changes the source. A confirmed retry returns the original saved version even if later work has advanced the article. Restoring an earlier version always creates a new draft and requires a reason and explicit confirmation. Imported historical snapshots retain their recorded labels without inventing an action actor or publication time. Private history is limited to administrators and doctors; patients and reception only see approved public content.
- Published clinic articles have stable `/library/:articleId` addresses and complete server-rendered reading pages. Search and pagination work without JavaScript. Public output includes only the current published version; withdrawal makes the same address unavailable and removes it from the sitemap. Attribution uses the recorded author and modified date, without inventing a publication date or independent approval. Public-library indexing requires an explicit production setting and a configured origin; private routes always remain noindex and require their existing access checks.
- A procedure may link to one invoice. Reception sees only its operational billing fields, while clinical discussion and outcomes remain protected. Staff explicitly enter agreed charges; descriptive estimates never become automatic charges. New links require a matching patient and a procedure that is not cancelled. Existing invoices survive later cancellation, and no automatic refund or adjustment is inferred. New invoices and recorded payments generate private patient activity.
- Administrators can correct invoice line items while preserving issued charges, identity, procedure linkage and dated before/after history. Totals cannot fall below net recorded payments. Removing all charges requires explicit confirmation and zero net paid, resulting in no-charge status. Entire erroneous payment entries may be reversed with a reason and explicit entry-error confirmation; originals remain immutable. These changes and reasons are shared with the patient and do not move or refund money.
- Payments use integer paise, reject overpayment and support safe retries. Recorded payment methods describe receipt elsewhere and do not initiate a charge.
- Consumable inventory is readable by staff and changed only by administrators. SKU and counting unit stay fixed. Receipts create immutable lots; usage and physical counts append attributed movements without overwriting balances or original events. Stock can never become negative. Catalogue edits preserve snapshots. Expired lots cannot be used, while physical counts may reconcile inactive or expired lots. Unknown expiry remains explicitly unrecorded. There is no pharmacy dispensing, patient linkage or automatic financial operation.
- Administrator reports separate appointment scheduled dates/current states, current corrected invoice amounts by original issue dates, nonreversed payment entries by original recording dates and expense incurred dates in Asia/Kolkata. Invoice-cohort outstanding is current, not a historical as-of balance. Daily rows are zero-filled; aggregate CSV exports omit patient identities and individual expense details. No metric is labeled profit, observed treatment adherence or clinical efficacy.
- Expense records use integer paise, a required retry key and server-derived authorship. Mistakes are voided with a reason while preserving the original entry. Current reports exclude voided expenses. This does not move money, reconcile a bank account, calculate tax or automatically enter a replacement.
- Existing patient records are linked to portal accounts only by an administrator after an explicit identity-verification confirmation. Email matching alone never grants record access.
- Password sign-in uses durable network and normalized-email limits with truthful retry times and bounded private audit evidence. Password-login failures do not reveal whether an account exists; duplicate registration retains its explicit conflict response. Registration and logout commit their account/session changes with audit evidence before changing cookies. These controls need no external service; verified contacts, breached-password screening and self-service recovery delivery remain separate future work.
- Staff patient and appointment lists and administrator audit history use bounded SQL-backed pages, with current-view counts and URL filters. Patient directory search includes only demographics. Appointment matching can use current patient contacts without rewriting booked-name snapshots. Audit filters search immutable recorded actor names and clinic dates. Existing patient-record and booking-selector APIs remain compatible; substring matching is not a full-text index.
- Staff may reserve custom visit lengths of 15–240 minutes in five-minute increments. Candidate starts use five-minute steps within a doctor session and the complete interval must fit availability and all retained resources. Future scheduled visits can change length or restore the doctor default with an internal reason and retained before/after history; date/time can move in the same transaction. A length-only change preserves booked fees, while an actual move follows the existing reschedule fee rule. Patients cannot override the length. Custom minutes do not imply clinical suitability, consent, a different charge or a changed procedure association.
- Staff attendance uses individual account IDs and server clock times. One entry can remain open per account; no effective nonvoid intervals may overlap. Administrators record missed closed intervals, correct times or void entries with reasons while preserving originals and actor history. Staff see their own records and administrator corrections; only administrators see the team. Timesheets use inclusive IST start dates and count the full elapsed seconds of completed entries in that cohort. Open/voided entries contribute no time. A 36-hour completed-entry limit is a data-entry constraint; older open entries require administrator correction or voiding. Attendance does not verify presence or infer absence, payroll, breaks, overtime or leave. Suspension and logout preserve open entries without fabricating clock-out.
- Staff can attach, replace or remove an eligible existing procedure appointment with a private reason and immutable history. The original plan link stays intact; explicit removal makes the effective link null. Targets are same-patient future scheduled visits. Completed/cancelled procedures and links to started/completed visits are read-only. Old bookings and their resources remain booked after reassignment/removal. Multiple plans can share a visit without inferring combined duration. Completion checks the effective visit and preserves its snapshot; patients see the current visit and completion snapshot but not private scheduling history. Booking a visit, patient agreement and billing remain separate actions.
- Optional authenticator sign-in requires a configured encryption key and explicit account enrollment. Setup requires the current password, saved recovery codes and a valid authenticator code. Password authentication alone does not create a full session for enrolled accounts. Recovery-code replacement keeps the existing batch active until confirmation; each accepted code can be consumed once. Password recovery retains the enrolled second factor. Initial-password accounts must finish password rotation before enrollment. Factor changes end other sessions; all-factor loss requires an audited local operator reset after identity verification. This release does not add mandatory clinic policies, external factor delivery, passkeys or remembered-device exceptions.
- The procedure catalogue starts empty. Linked clinicians author immutable versions and explicitly publish the latest draft; a newer draft leaves the last publication usable. Administrators archive/reactivate templates without changing prior patient plans. Selection always requires patient-specific review and an explicit indication. Final plan wording and safe source attribution are saved independently; later catalogue changes cannot alter patient decisions or summaries. Internal review reasons, drafts and command receipts stay private. Publication records a clinician review, not independent approval or a validated consent process.
- The product remains useful without external integrations. Unconfigured providers are displayed as unavailable, without fake success states.

## Remaining substantial releases

1. Provider-backed reminders with explicit delivery results. Clinical review, assignable ownership, configurable escalation policies, automatic local checks and in-app notice history are implemented.
2. Deeper clinic operations: actual refund handling and complete record-release and retention workflows. Procedure-to-invoice linkage, confirmed waitlist booking, expenses, aggregate reporting, patient-facing care-summary export, consumable inventory, appointment-linked room/equipment reservations, staff attendance, custom visit lengths, finite recurring appointment series, scheduling existing procedure plans and versioned procedure templates are implemented.
3. Verified contact methods, self-service recovery delivery and deployment-specific clinic onboarding. Optional authenticator MFA and single-use recovery codes are implemented.
4. Validated clinician workbench modules with approved sources, version history and clinical review; independent consent categories after their purpose and governance are defined.
5. Multi-clinic isolation and scalable storage/operations once deployment requirements are known.

These are feature groups suitable for meaningful commits. They are not represented as complete in the current release.

# Product decisions and concept coverage

The supplied concept is source material, not an instruction to make unsupported medical or commercial claims. Its unresolved items remain unresolved unless this implementation establishes a concrete operational behavior.

## Decisions made for these releases

- One deployed clinic has four explicit roles: administrator, doctor, receptionist and patient. Doctor accounts are tied to professional profiles. Administrators can manage clinic operations, but must also have a linked doctor profile to author clinical notes or care plans.
- Local accounts avoid dependency on an external identity provider. Production never accepts a demo-seeded database. New administrator-provisioned staff and patient portal accounts must replace their initial password before accessing private workspace data. Self-registered patients choose their own password during registration.
- Administrators can suspend or restore accounts without deleting care records. Role, doctor-profile and active-status changes end the affected account’s sessions and invalidate its outstanding recovery grants. Patient accounts cannot be converted into staff accounts or relinked to another patient. At least one active administrator must remain, and administrators cannot suspend themselves.
- Account holders can inspect their active sessions and end other sessions. Password changes keep the current session and end the others. Sessions have a fixed twelve-hour lifetime; browser labels are descriptive and are not verified device identities.
- Recovery is a clinic-assisted process: an administrator confirms identity and obtains a one-use link that expires after thirty minutes. The administrator shares it privately outside the app. Only the token digest is stored. Redeeming the link changes the password, ends all prior sessions and requires a fresh sign-in. No email or SMS is sent automatically, and the identity-confirmation checkbox is not automated identity verification.
- Bookable hours come from each doctor’s weekly schedule. The default is Monday–Saturday, 09:00–18:00 IST, in thirty-minute slots; it can be replaced with other days, multiple sessions, mode-specific hours and 15/20/30/45/60-minute appointment lengths. The current booking horizon is 180 days. The public clinic-hours description is informational and does not configure availability.
- Slots are authoritative on the server. Transactional overlap checks protect both doctor and patient time, including appointments with different lengths. Time off can cover one doctor or the entire clinic, for full days or a repeated daily time interval. A schedule change or closure that invalidates future bookings is rejected; it never automatically cancels those bookings.
- Appointment duration is recorded with the booking. Changing weekly availability preserves existing appointments and their duration. Explicitly rescheduling an appointment selects a current slot and adopts the selected doctor’s current appointment length. Calendar exports use the recorded length. Earlier records without a duration are interpreted as thirty minutes.
- Appointment states follow a defined transition graph. Patients may cancel or reschedule their own scheduled appointments; they cannot progress clinic states.
- Internal visit notes are clinician-only. Patients receive their structured care plans and shared history, images, messages, appointments and invoices.
- Clinical instructions are entered by a clinician. No medication recommendation, clinical probability, image-based score or future-outcome image is generated.
- Check-ins are explicitly patient-reported. One per care plan/day can be corrected; changes are audited. They are not presented as observed adherence or clinical efficacy.
- Image agreement covers private treatment use only. There is no automatic publishing path from a clinical image to the public website.
- Draft knowledge is private. Publication requires an explicit clinician review action. Editing published material returns it to draft unless it is explicitly approved again.
- Payments use integer paise, reject overpayment and support safe retries. Recorded payment methods describe receipt elsewhere and do not initiate a charge.
- Existing patient records are linked to portal accounts only by an administrator after an explicit identity-verification confirmation. Email matching alone never grants record access.
- The product remains useful without external integrations. Unconfigured providers are displayed as unavailable, without fake success states.

## Remaining substantial releases

1. Structured investigations and procedure records, private document uploads and explicit amendment histories.
2. Follow-up review workflow: a clinician worklist for patient-reported changes, review ownership and outcomes, and an in-app activity inbox. External reminder delivery remains a separate integration.
3. Verified contact methods, MFA, self-service recovery delivery and deployment-specific clinic onboarding.
4. Validated clinician workbench modules with approved sources, version history and clinical review; independent consent categories after their purpose and governance are defined.
5. Multi-clinic isolation and scalable storage/operations once deployment requirements are known.

These are feature groups suitable for meaningful commits. They are not represented as complete in the current release.
